Lead · South America

Strong quake shakes Peru’s Andes; Lima rushes into the streets

A magnitude 6.8 earthquake struck central Peru at 00:09 GMT, centred in the Andes near Junín. Tremors were felt across Lima, sending residents outdoors. Peru’s Geophysical Institute reported no damage or casualties, and no tsunami threat was issued.

Lead · Climate

COP31 deadlocked as talks run into overtime

Climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, ran through the night and were suspended with no deal. Nearly 200 countries are split over whether the final text names fossil fuels — a draft dropped all mention of them — and over adaptation finance, after a $120bn target was cut. For the first time, a COP text acknowledges the 1.5°C goal has been missed.
